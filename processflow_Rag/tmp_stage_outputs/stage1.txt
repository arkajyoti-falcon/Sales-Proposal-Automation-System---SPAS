### Process Flow

#### Infeed System
Shipments are loaded onto the infeed conveyor and ascend to a higher level via an inclined conveyor, where they are distributed to the induction area. This initial step ensures that all shipments are properly positioned for further processing.

#### Distribution Loop
Once the shipments are within the distribution loop, they are temporarily stored before being inducted into the system. This loop system plays a crucial role in managing the flow of shipments, ensuring that they are efficiently processed.

#### Induction
The operator picks and positions each shipment on the induct line, ensuring that the shipment is properly aligned and that its barcode is facing upwards. The feedlines then automatically induct the shipments onto the Cross-Belt Sorter Loop. This process combines the precision of manual handling with the efficiency of automated induction, streamlining the sorting process.

#### Loop CBS
Once the shipments are on the Cross-Belt Sorter Loop, they are sorted based on their characteristics, utilizing the data provided by the sorting logic. This logic enables efficient sorting of shipments into their respective collection points. The system's design and the sorting logic work together to ensure accurate and timely processing of all shipments.

#### Output Chutes
There are a total of 43 chutes present in the system, categorized into three main types:
- **Big Parcel Chutes**: 26 chutes are designed to handle larger shipments, ensuring that these items are properly sorted and directed to their next destination.
- **Irregular Chutes**: 12 chutes are designed to handle irregularly shaped or sized shipments, providing a flexible solution for items that do not fit standard dimensions.
- **Other Chutes**: 5 chutes are designated for other types of shipments, allowing for a comprehensive sorting process that accommodates a wide range of items.

#### Put To Light System
The system also features 122 Put To Light locations, which facilitate efficient sorting and processing of the shipments. At these locations, operators can efficiently consolidate shipments, ensuring accurate and timely processing.

#### Bag Takeaway Conveyor
After sorting, shipments are manually loaded onto a bag takeaway conveyor, which transports bags containing shipments to their next destination. This step ensures a smooth and efficient process, handling bags with precision and care.
